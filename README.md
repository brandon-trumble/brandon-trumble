### About Me

I'm a developer based in Ontario, currently completing my studies at Georgian College (graduating August 2026). By day, I work as a Full Stack Engineer building out production backend architectures. By night, I'm split between **Go** — goroutines, channels, and distributed service design — and **Rust**, where I'm working through ownership, lifetimes, and fearless concurrency to get closer to the metal.

Two languages, one goal: services that stay fast and correct under load.
